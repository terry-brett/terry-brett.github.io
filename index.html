GOOD TO KNOW

A few sound answers.

What does Volu do?

Volu provides automated Android per-app volume control. It remembers your preferred sound levels for individual apps and automatically applies them whenever you open an app, preventing sudden loud bursts of sound.

Does stock Android support per-app volume control?

No. Stock Android only provides a single global media volume slider. Volu bridges this gap by detecting which app is in the foreground and applying your preset volume automatically without requiring root.

How does Volu adjust volume for each app?

Volu uses Android's standard Usage Access permission to detect when a configured app enters the foreground, instantly and smoothly setting the media stream to your desired percentage without manual adjustment.

Does Volu require root or ADB permissions?

No. Volu does not require root, custom ROMs, or ADB setup. It functions right out of the box on standard Android devices running Android 8.0+ using the standard Usage Access permission.

Can I trigger volume profiles with Wi-Fi or Bluetooth?

Yes. Volu allows you to bind sound profiles (such as Home, Work, Gym, or Car) to specific Wi-Fi SSIDs, Bluetooth devices, wired headphones, or time schedules.
