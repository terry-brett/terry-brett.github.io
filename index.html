Per-app volume control for Android. Automatically set the perfect volume for every app the moment you open it.

RulesManage App Volumes

Easily set specific volume levels for individual apps. Whether you want Netflix loud or Instagram quiet, Volu instantly applies your chosen volume the moment you open the app.

ApplyFine-tuned Control

Choose exactly when rules take effect. Apply them once per app switch, once per screen session, or only when conditions are met. Stay in complete control of your audio experience.

ProfilesContextual Sound Profiles

Set custom audio levels for Home, Work, or the Gym. Volu seamlessly adapts your sound when you connect to Wi-Fi, plug in headphones, start Android Auto, or follow your daily schedule.

ConditionsContext Aware

Make your rules smarter by binding them to specific Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices, or schedules. Mute automatically at work, or turn up the volume when connected to your home network or car Bluetooth.

Global RulesCatch-All Settings

Set a default behavior for all other apps. Apply a global rule when you connect to a specific Wi-Fi network or enter a time schedule, ensuring you're never caught off guard.
