GOOD TO KNOW

A few sound answers.

What does Volu do?

Volu sets your Android phone’s volume to your chosen level when you open an app. Create a rule for each app, then let Volu handle the adjustments automatically.

Can I use different rules at home and at work?

Yes. Profiles let you group settings for places and situations like Home, Work and the Gym. Use Wi-Fi, connected devices or schedules to decide when rules take effect.

What happens to apps without a volume rule?

Default volume settings let you choose the behavior for apps that don’t have their own per-app rule.

When will the volume change?

You can choose to apply a rule each time you switch to an app, once per screen session, or when its triggers are met.
